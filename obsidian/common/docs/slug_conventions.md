# Slug Conventions

## Format

- lowercase
- words separated by hyphens
- ASCII only unless there is a clear editorial reason otherwise

## Rules

- Keep slugs stable after publication or pipeline use.
- Avoid dates unless the date is semantically necessary.
- Avoid duplicate slugs within the same vault.
- Prefer concise descriptive phrases over opaque IDs.

## Examples

- `arrival-in-yogya`
- `freebergs-last-flight`
- `photo-spread-maguwo`
